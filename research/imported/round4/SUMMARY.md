## Headline discoveries

**Meier / German**
- **Henry J. Meier died 20 Dec 1950, not 1940.** The 1950 census shows him alive, aged 83, at 16 Barney St, Wilkes-Barre. The PA death index lists "Harry J Meier", Wilkes-Barre, 12/20/50, file 104173. This explains why round 2 found nothing for 1939–41, and it rules out the Huntington, NY Henry who died in 1931.
- **Frederick Meier was born 11 Jan 1862, before his parents married.** The Munzingen register records Friedrich Mayer formally acknowledging him as his son.
- **Theresa Meier was born 10 Sep 1868, not 1872.** She is the "Clarissa" of the 1870 census and the "Fressa" of 1880, so record H0017 is a duplicate of her. Her birth date also proves the family was in Hazleton by September 1868.
- **The Levittown Meiers descend from Fred A. Meier Sr.'s son Gerald.** Fred's other son, Staff Sgt. Harry Meier, was killed in Italy in 1943.

**Irish**
- **Death-certificate file numbers were found for eight Irish-line ancestors.** They are Edward McGuire (1907), James McGuire (1927), Anna C. Jones McGuire (1925), Catherine Murphy Jones (1931), Anna Higgins Meier (1951), Andrew H. Heffernan (1919), Mary Connole Heffernan (1928) and Thomas F. Heffernan (1951).
- **James McGuire's 1927 marriage application for his son gives James's birthplace as Danville.** That ties him to the 1870 Edward McGuire household without relying on the FamilySearch tree.
- **Several Ancestry imports are biologically impossible.** Peter McAvoy is only 12 years older than his supposed son. Richard Jones and Catherine Williams are only 12–14 years older than theirs. Thomas Comerford is too young to be Alice's father. The 11-person Delaware/Fermanagh McGuire cluster does not match the documented family.
- **New photos and records for the Heffernans.** A 1917 Library of Congress photo shows Capt. Leo G. Heffernan at Wilbur Wright Field. A 1906 portrait of Thomas F. Heffernan comes with a caption saying he chaired the Luzerne County Republican Committee in 1903–05.

**Pringle / Pennsylvania / Huguenot / Palatine**
- **Thomas Davenport's will of 10 Oct 1746 lists Charity as an unmarried minor child of his second wife, Elizabeth.** That dates her marriage to Lamoreaux to 1746–52, which makes Ensign Thomas Lamoreaux's "about 1745" birth unlikely.
- **Jean Lamoreaux held three enslaved people in the 1790 New Cornwall census.** His next-door neighbours were Oliver Davenport and David Prindle.
- **The Grub line has two wives merged into one record.** Peter Grub's first wife, Gretha Catharina, and his second wife, Elisabetha Catharina Jung (married 1694, died 1734), are both in record H0078. Either way, the Jungs remain ancestors through Maria Sara Jung.
- **The Doll line goes back a generation.** The 1704 Ulmet marriage names Hans Peter Doll of Erzweiler and Joh. Peter Schuch as the next generation.
- **Collateral relatives with stories:**
  - Caleb A. Pringle served in the 52nd PA and died in Missouri in 1919.
  - George L. Pringle was killed by lightning in 1886.
  - Col. George P. Ransom was fined one cent.
  - In 1935 the pavilion at Croop's Glen collapsed.

**Long Island colonial**
- **"Justice Adam Smith" is ruled out as James Smith's father.** Pelletreau (1898, p. 46) prints Adam's 1704 will, which leaves everything to "his only son Edmund".
- **A second printed source supports Temperance Smith's parents.** Weygant's *Family Record* (1897, pp. 144–145) names James Smith and Jerusha (Topping) as her parents. That is older than the 1968 Tuttle book.
- **A new direct ancestor: Deliverance King** (baptized Salem 1641, NEW-501). She was the first wife of John Tuthill (1635–1717) and mother of John Jr.
- **The tree has the wrong wife for James Tuthill.** "Hannah Reeve" was his brother Joshua's wife; James married Rachel Browne. Separately, the Youngs/Horne block of about 14 people is not ancestral, and "Mary Youngs" was really Mary (unknown) Wells Mapes.
- **Cousin paths were checked generation by generation in printed genealogies.** These cover Benjamin Harrison, Anna Symmes Harrison, Julia Gardiner Tyler, William Floyd, Theodorus Bailey, Sen. John Smith, Mayor C.V.W. Lawrence and others. James Clinch Smith's relationship is revised to 5th cousin 5x removed, via Job Smith.
- **Public-domain portraits** of several of these cousins were downloaded.

**Italian**
- **Helen Ferlaino was baptized Maria Filomena** at San Mango d'Aquino on 9 Oct 1894, daughter of Leopoldo Ferlaino and Giuseppina Fiorillo. I read the register image. She died 14 Mar 1965, and "Filomena Ferlaino" in the tree is the same woman.
- **Leopoldo Ferlaino (baptized 1871) died at Dickson City on 2 Nov 1915.** His wife, Giuseppina "Josephine Farino", died at Scranton in 1943.
- **Helen's parents were related to each other.** Two Ferlaino brothers and two Moraca siblings appear in both of their pedigrees.
- **About 80 San Mango ancestors (1650–1900) now have indexed church or civil entries**, and the Fiorillos are traced to Aiello Calabro.
- **PA death-index entries for the Avellino line.** John Petriello (indexed as Giovanni J Petrillo) died 1939, Frank Giannetta died 1934, and Mariano Giannetta died 1941.
- **James Petriello's 1940 draft card** confirms his birth on 6 Dec 1910 in Scranton and names his wife, Mollie.
- **Grace Petrillo DiBiasi's 1943 "naturalization" was a repatriation.** She had lost her citizenship by marrying an alien.
- **The Petrillo children need cleaning up.** Joseph Petrillo is probably a phantom record, and Katie is probably Grace.

## What went wrong or was limited

- **Blocked sites:** Ancestry, Find a Grave, BillionGraves, Antenati and the Irish registers (irishgenealogy.ie, registers.nli.ie) were blocked, as were FamilySearch record search, its ark pages and its Italian images. NYS Archives failed on a certificate error, Ellis Island needs a login, and NARA AAD and GenealogyBank were also blocked. Wikipedia's API rate-limited some lookups; workers used page fetches instead.
- **What still worked:** the FamilySearch published-tree data and its attached US record images, the PA death-index PDFs, archive.org full texts, the San Mango volunteer indexes, LOC images and the WikiTree API.
- **Light passes:** about 28 English and tier-2 Long Island people got only a light pass and are marked nothing_new. Many deep German and tier-2 Pringle facts come only from the FamilySearch tree, and are marked derivative.
- **Lines with little new:** the Dasà (Cognetta/Lamanna) and Aiello lines yielded almost nothing because Antenati was blocked. Irish parish sources could not be searched at all.
- **Missing index volumes:** I could not find the PA death-index PDFs for 1933, 1935 (P) and 1950 (G).

## How to use this folder

- `people/<id>.json`: one file per person, holding only new or changed information, in the site's field names.
- `media_manifest.csv`: every media item. `downloaded=true` rows have a file at `localPath`.
- `progress.json`: resume state. Everything is complete. `_work/` holds the derived roster, the per-line worklists and logs, the worker brief, and `merge.js`, which rebuilds the top-level files from the per-line files.
