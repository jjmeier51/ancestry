# meiertree.com research, round 5: living members (2026-10-08)

## What was done
- **Worklist:** every person on meiertree.com without a death record who is living or presumed living: 52 people (the 26 round-4 skipped as living, plus the 26 round 4 found to be living). The live `data/family.js` was re-checked on 2026-10-08; no one had been added since round 4.
- **Method:** five parallel research passes by family group (John's household; Danny/Jamie/Kathy; the other Meier siblings and McGuire aunts; Petriello; Cognetti), using the round-4 format and evidence rules: two matching identifiers before anything is attached, only URLs actually loaded, and sources classified.
- **Totals:** 52 person files (22 researched, 22 nothing new, 5 found to be dead, 3 children limited to name only); 154 source citations; 35 media items (2 downloaded); 22 new people; 8 stories; 31 leads.

## Privacy rule applied (John can widen it)
- **Collected:** what a family-history site would publish: public photos and media, news articles, sports records, awards, obituaries of relatives that name the person, published marriage and engagement announcements, public professional and community profiles, and anything notable.
- **Not recorded:** home addresses, phone numbers, emails, financial or court data, full birth dates, data-broker results (those sites were not used), DNA, medical details, and criminal records.
- **Children:** name and relationship only. Children of unknown age were treated as minors: Luca Meier, and Jack and Mollie Petriello. The four Russo children's ages are unknown, so they got one cautious name check each and nothing more.
- **Photos:** modern photos are copyrighted, so they are recorded with links but not downloaded. Only public-domain or CC items were saved.
