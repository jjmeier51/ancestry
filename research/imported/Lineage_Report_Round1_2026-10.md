# Johnny Meier's Whole Lineage: Standouts, Cousins and Regional Ties

*Research completed 7 Oct 2026. It builds on John's two earlier reports ("Old Records Reshape Johnny Meier's Family Tree" and "Family ties to Hauppauge") and the Bull Smith lineage PDF, which override the Ancestry tree where they conflict. The Ancestry tree itself came from the 49 screenshots in the project's Google Drive folder. Detailed notes with a source link on every claim are in [research/notes/](/mnt/project-files/research/notes/). Labels: **confirmed** (a record or well-sourced compilation states it), **probable** (strong fit, one document short), **speculative** (a lead).*

## The short version

- **Northeastern Pennsylvania is the family's center of gravity, on both sides.** Every branch that reached America in the last 230 years settled in the anthracite valleys:
  - **The Meiers** were in Hazleton, then Wilkes-Barre.
  - **The Pringle, Lamoreaux, Croup, Scott and Heffernan lines** were in Plymouth, Kingston and Larksville.
  - **The mother's Italian lines** (Petriello, Cognetti, Ferlaino) were in Scranton and Dunmore.
- **Long Island is the deep root.** It is entirely colonial, from 1640 to about 1760, through Smithtown, Southampton, Southold, Setauket and Moriches. No 19th- or 20th-century Long Island tie turned up for any line.
- **Virginia has no ancestor, only cousins.** No direct ancestor in any line lived in Virginia. The one genuine Virginia blood tie is new: **First Lady Julia Gardiner Tyler**, wife of President John Tyler, and her Virginia-born Tyler children are cousins through Bull Smith.
- **The closest notable relatives are on the mother's side.** Johnny's grandmother Mary Cognetti Petriello had two brothers of note:
  - **Anthony R. Cognetti** was president of Scranton City Council.
  - **Joseph F. Cognetti** was a WWII Army Air Corps veteran inducted into the Candy Hall of Fame.
- **The tree needs several new fixes on top of the 30 already flagged.** The biggest:
  - Jerusha Topping's parents are wrong, so the whole Sayre branch comes off.
  - The Youngs ancestry is unproven.
  - "Captain" Johann C. Doll was not a captain.
  - The Italian tree has a pair of duplicated parents.

### Long Island (colonial only)
- **Smithtown and Nissequogue:** Bull Smith and Job Smith's home lots. James Smith's 1736 tract bordered the Hauppauge estate.
- **Moriches:** James Smith & Jerusha Topping settled here.
- **Southampton, Sagaponack and Bridgehampton:** the Toppings. Bull Smith's first home lot was on Southampton's main street.
- **Southold, Cutchogue and Mattituck:** William Wells and the Tuthills.
- **Setauket:** John Thompson's home lot faced the village green.
- **Cousin sites:** Gardiner's Island and East Hampton (Julia Tyler's birthplace); Sagtikos Manor (the Thompsons); Hauppauge (the Smith judges).
- **No later tie found:** nothing places the Meiers, McGuires or Petriellos on Long Island in the 1900s. If the family moved there, Kathryn (McGuire) Meier's 1970 death in the New York State Death Index is the first place to check.

### Virginia (cousins only)
- **Genuine blood tie:** the Tylers of Sherwood Forest, Charles City County, through Julia Gardiner Tyler.
- **Not blood ties:**
  - The Virginia Harrisons of Berkeley Plantation. Benjamin Harrison is kin only through his Long Island grandmother Anna Symmes.
  - Stephen Sayre, who died in Brandon, Virginia; the Sayre line is not Johnny's.
  - Roger Ludlow and Col. Richard Townley, both in-laws only.
  - A Bretten Doll family that moved to Augusta County, Virginia. It is a different family that online trees often merge with Johnny's Dolls.
- **No direct ancestor, and no Revolutionary service in Virginia, was found in any line.**

## 3. Corrections to add to the tree

These come on top of the 30 already listed in the earlier report.

**Colonial lines**
- **3a. Jerusha Topping's parents.** They were **Elnathan Topping (1664–1751) & Mary**, not Josiah Topping & Hannah Sayre. Howell (1887) lists a Jerusha in Elnathan's will, and the Sayre genealogy gives Josiah & Hannah only two sons. Remove Josiah Topping, Hannah Sayre, John Topping (1636–1686) and the Sayres. This also fixes the date problem the Hauppauge report raised. *Probable.*
- **3b. Mary "Youngs," wife of William Wells.** Both standard genealogies (Hayes 1878, Youngs 1907) reject her as Rev. John Youngs's daughter; that daughter married Edward Petty. Mark Rev. John Youngs, Vicar Christopher Youngs and the "Horne" ancestors as **unproven**.
- **3c. William Wells's parents.** "Rev. William Wells to the Queen of Denmark" garbles a Norwich prebendary who was chaplain to Queen Anne of Denmark. He is a possible father, by family tradition only. The "Anne Elizabeth Hunt Kimball" mother is unsupported.
- **3d. Bull Smith's parents.** "Sir Samuel Smith & Rebecca Buswell" are unsupported, so remove them. His wife Sarah Hammond was the daughter of **William Hammond of Watertown, MA**, a newly identified 11th-great-grandfather.
- **3e. Hannah Brewster** (wife of John Thompson) was a daughter of Francis Brewster of New Haven. She was **not** of the Mayflower Brewster family.
- **3f. Tree versus PDF.** The tree shows "Justice Adam Smith" as James Smith's father; the PDF is right that it was **Job Smith**.

**Pennsylvania lines**
- **3g. "Johann C. Doll, Captain."** He is **Johann Casper Doll** of Ulmet (bapt. 1722, d. 1793, Plainfield Twp.) and was not a captain. The captaincy, the 1934 monument and the flag photo belong to his nephew Capt. Casper Dull (1748–1829). Whether Anna Maria Doll was Casper's daughter is unproven.
- **3h. Add missing ancestors.**
  - Maria Elisabetha Christmann (1729–1815), wife of Andrew Grub.
  - Susan Croup Shupp, Elizabeth's sister, who independently confirms Frederick & Mary (Young) Croup.
- **3i. Scott dates.**
  - Stephen Scott was born c.1797 or earlier (not 1808) and died 1855.
  - Julia Ann was born c.1799, not 1809.
  - Both were born in Connecticut.
  - The surname "Watson" is unsourced.

**Meier and McGuire**
- **3j. Middle initials and dates.**
  - William is **William F.** Meier.
  - James is **James C.** Meier, born 13 Jul 1928 (probable).
  - Kathryn F. McGuire was born 22 Aug 1930 to **Francis McGuire & Elizabeth McAvoy** (probable). Add them.
- **3k. Henry J. Meier's 1940 death** is not in the Pennsylvania index under any spelling. Either he died out of state or the year is wrong.

**Italian lines**
- **3l. Claudia Di Vivo** has been given her husband Claudio Gialanella's parents. Remove them.
- **3m. Cognetti siblings.**
  - The two "Ralph Cognetti 1913" entries are one man: Ralph A. Cognetti (7 Aug 1913 – 2009).
  - "Levyso" is **Leo S. Cognetti** (c.1929–2019).
  - Joseph was born in 1919, not 1918.
- **3n. Mollie Petriello** (b. 1911) is James Petriello's wife, not Mary Cognetti. Her maiden name is unknown.
- **3o. Helen Cognetti's maiden name conflicts.** The tree says **Ferlaino**; Anthony Cognetti's 2008 obituary says "Helen **Farina** Cognetti." The whole Fiorillo/Morrello/Guercio pedigree hangs on this.
- **3p. Identical dates and dubious attachments.**
  - Several couples share identical dates (Dimarino/Magnotta 1760–1808, D'Ambrosio/Giordano d. 1819), which look copied.
  - The "Lucera, Foggia" record on Domenico Di Biase is probably attached to the wrong man. The family was from Guardia Lombardi in Avellino.

## 4. The lines researched for the first time

**The Petriello line came from Guardia Lombardi, Avellino.**
- **Confirmed:** Giovanni "John" Petriello was born there on 25 Oct 1869, son of Pietro Petriello & Grazia di Marino, and married Maria Antonia DiBiasi. He came to the US about 1889–95 and died in Scranton in 1939. James Petriello (1911–1998) is probably their son "James Petrillo."
- **Probable:** the Di Biase, Siconolfi, Gialanella, Magnotta and Di Marino ancestors were from the same town. Gialanella, Magnotta and Siconolfi all have Guardia as their top place of origin, and Guardia even has a hamlet called Case Siconolfi.

**The Cognetta line probably came from Dasà, now in Vibo Valentia province, Calabria.**
- **Probable:** a Nicola Cognetta married to a Cannatello born in Dasà in 1818 matches the tree's Nicola Cognetta & Mariantonia Cannatello.
- **Confirmed:** Frank Cognetti was in Scranton by 1913.

**The Ferlaino and Fiorillo line probably came from San Mango d'Aquino, Catanzaro.**
- **Probable:** five of the branch's surnames (Ferlaino, Fiorillo, Colosimo, Fata, Marsico) are typical of the town.
- **Confirmed:** a Ferlaino household at 1306 Diamond Ave, Scranton, took in boarders from San Mango.
- **Not identified:** the black coat of arms with a silver lion on the Fiorillo profiles matches none of the 35 nearby comuni checked. It may have been copied from another family's tree.

**Meier, Higgins and McGuire:** see 2 and 3j–3k. No notable relative was proven in these lines. Anna Higgins's origin is still unknown.

**Croup/Grub, Young and Doll:**
- **Confirmed:** these were Palatine Germans from Jettenbach and Ulmet, a few kilometres apart in the Kusel district. They came through Philadelphia and Easton, and Frederick Croup moved to the Hunlock's Creek/Nanticoke river flats about 1800.
- **Probable:** the Croop sawmills, and later the Croop's Glen amusement park, descend from Frederick's nephews' line, not his own.

## 5. Records that would settle the most

1. **PA death certificates** with known file numbers: Frederick Meier 1913 #14339 and Mary A. Meier 1911 #113353 (both Hazleton), and William F. Meier 1963 #071200. They should give Frederick's German birthplace, his parents, and William's parents, which tests the Henry J. Meier–Anna Higgins link.
2. **The Lackawanna County marriage license** of Frank Cognetti & Helen (c.1912–13). It settles Ferlaino vs. Farina.
3. **Kathryn Meier's 1970 death record:** the New York index if she died there, otherwise a PA certificate request. Her SS-5 application should name her birthplace and parents.
4. **Antenati civil records** for Guardia Lombardi (John Petriello's 1869 birth act), San Mango d'Aquino (the Ferlaino × Fiorillo marriage and Helen's birth) and Dasà (Giovanni Cognetta's 1855 birth).
5. **The colonial gaps:**
   - The 1967 F. K. Smith genealogy entry for James Smith, and Elnathan Topping's 1751 will, to settle Temperance Smith's parents and the Topping generation.
   - Akerly and Tuttle compilations, to settle Capt. John Tuttle's father.
6. **The SAR/DAR databases**, searched by hand in a browser (automated searches were blocked), for Casper Doll, John Young and Andrew Grub. Patriotic service such as an oath of allegiance or supplies would also count.

*Access limits this round: Ancestry, Find a Grave, FamilySearch record search, Antenati, Geni and Legacy all blocked automated access. Several findings therefore rest on public indexes and search-engine extracts, and are labelled probable rather than confirmed. Living people were not researched beyond the names already in the tree.*
